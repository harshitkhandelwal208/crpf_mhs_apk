# Clarifying questions

Ask the minimum question needed to choose safe support. Use open, non-accusatory language and explain why a question matters when it could feel intrusive.

## Useful questions

- “What has changed, and for how long?”
- “How is this affecting sleep, concentration, appetite, or day-to-day functioning?”
- “Is this connected mainly to duty, home, health, or something else?”
- “What support have you already tried?”
- “Would you prefer self-help information, a confidential welfare check-in, or clinical support?”
- “Are you safe right now?”

## Data-quality clarification

When an HR-based alert is involved, ask a welfare officer to verify that the data is current, consented, and relevant. Do not ask the person to justify leave, transfers, or medical information to an AI system. A model-generated flag should be framed as a prompt for human review, never as a conclusion.
